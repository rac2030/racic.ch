## Examples

- "I am currently out of the office and will be back never. Just kidding, I'll be back on Monday."
- "Thank you for your email. I am out of the office until further notice. If this is urgent, please contact someone who cares."
- "I'm not here right now. Please leave a message at the beep. Just kidding, I'll read your email when I get back."
- "I am currently on vacation. I will have limited access to email. If this is urgent, please contact my backup — ha, just kidding, I don't have one."
