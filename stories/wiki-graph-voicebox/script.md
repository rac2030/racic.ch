## Narration (approved)

### Segment 1 — Hook
> I built a knowledge graph. On my portfolio website. Because regular links were just too boring.

### Segment 2 — Setup
> Every wiki article becomes a node in this floating constellation. Same category, same color. Shared tags get wired up into the net.

### Segment 3 — Deep dive: live filters
> But here's the genuinely cool part — it respects your filters. I click the security category and boom, everything else vanishes.

### Segment 4 — Deep dive: bookmark diamonds
> And watch the teal diamonds. Those are bookmark collections. They only stay while one of their connected articles is still visible.

### Segment 5 — Deep dive: double-click navigation
> Double click any node and you jump straight to the article. Pure canvas. No graph library — just physics, vibes, and hope.

### Segment 6 — Punchline 1
> The whole thing settles after five seconds. Because simulating gravity forever? That would melt my laptop.

### Segment 7 — Punchline 2: the bug the tests caught
> Fun fact: the tests caught a real bug. Bookmarks lingered after their articles were filtered out. The test was right. I was very wrong.

### Segment 8 — Outro
> Anyway, that's my knowledge graph. One of five features I built when literally nobody asked me to.
